Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent operator of prudent-plumbing.com may collect, use, share and protect information when you visit this website or contact our plumbing referral enquiry line. We are not the former Prudent Plumbing business and are not a plumbing contractor.

This policy is intended to cover our present enquiry and voicemail process and potential future call routing or referrals. It does not mean that every optional feature described is currently in operation.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site and telephone line, we may receive:

  • Enquiry details: your name, telephone number, city or location, details of your plumbing issue and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Call-related information: calling number, date, time, duration, routing and call-status information, and voicemail recordings or recordings of calls when enabled and lawful.
  • Basic website technical data: IP address, device and browser information, pages visited and referral information if this is collected by our hosting provider or any analytics service we later enable.
  • Referral outcomes: limited information about whether a call was connected, accepted or otherwise qualified, where a referral or call-tracking arrangement is used.

Do not include payment-card details, Social Security numbers, medical details or other sensitive information in a voicemail or enquiry.

How we use information

We may use information to receive and evaluate plumbing enquiries, respond where practicable, route or refer enquiries to independent providers, prevent misuse or fraud, maintain and improve the website and call flow, document consent, comply with legal requirements, and resolve complaints or disputes.

Where a commercial referral arrangement applies, call and enquiry data may be used to determine whether a referral qualifies for compensation.

Calls, voicemail and recording

Incoming calls may first play an automated message explaining the independent referral nature of the service. Callers may be asked to press a key to continue, leave a voicemail, or be connected to an independent provider if routing is enabled.

Voicemails are recorded. Other calls may be monitored or recorded for enquiry handling, quality review, verification or fraud prevention only where permitted by applicable law and subject to any required advance notice and consent. California law can require the consent of all parties before recording a confidential telephone conversation.

If you do not want a voicemail to be recorded, do not leave a message. Where a call-recording notice is provided, you may end the call rather than continue. Website text by itself is not a substitute for consent that the law may require during the call.

Who may receive information

We may disclose relevant enquiry details to independent plumbing service providers or referral partners to help handle a request, to communications and hosting providers (including the telephone platform used to receive calls), and to service providers helping operate or secure the website. We may disclose information if legally required or to protect against fraud or misuse.

Independent providers are separate businesses, and their handling of data and provision of services may be governed by their own notices and terms. We do not publish or promise a particular list of partners because these relationships may change.

We may receive compensation for qualified calls or referrals. We do not describe that compensation as a charge automatically imposed on callers.

Cookies and technical services

The website uses standard hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may process server logs and technical information needed to deliver and secure the site. If analytics, cookies or additional advertising or call-tracking technology are introduced, the website and this policy should be updated and any required choices or notices provided before use. We do not claim that no technical information is processed.

Data retention and security

Information is kept for as long as reasonably needed for enquiry handling, referral verification, service operation, security, legal compliance or dispute resolution, and then deleted or de-identified where appropriate. Different providers may apply their own retention settings and periods. No internet or telephone service can guarantee absolute security.

Privacy choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection or concern, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention obligations. California residents may have additional rights under California privacy law where its requirements apply to the operator. We will assess requests in light of the laws that apply rather than promising rights or procedures that may not apply in every case.

We do not intentionally solicit information from children. The website and telephone line are intended for adults seeking information about household plumbing issues.

Service providers and international processing

The website operator may use service providers based in the United States or other countries, including communications, hosting or administrative platforms. Your information may therefore be accessed or processed outside your state or country, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the website, phone flow or applicable rules change. The date at the top identifies the latest published version. Material changes to recording or referral practices should also be reflected in relevant call announcements and operational settings.
